# PRD: Daily activity digest

## Problem

Members do not return to the product between meetings, so workspace activity goes unread and decisions stall waiting on people who never saw them.

## What we want

Each member receives one digest per day summarizing workspace activity. The workspace chooses what time of day the digest goes out, because the right time differs by team — a 9am digest is useless to a team that starts at noon.

The send time is expressed in the workspace's own timezone, since that is how an admin thinks about it when they configure it.

## Out of scope

- Per-member digest scheduling.
- Weekly or monthly digests.
